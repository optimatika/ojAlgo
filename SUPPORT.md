# Support

## Free

- **Questions and discussion** — [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/optimatika/ojAlgo/discussions)
- **Bugs, or any issue with existing code** — [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/optimatika/ojAlgo/issues)

Both are public. Your question — and whatever it reveals about what you are
building — is visible to everyone, and stays that way.

Answers come from whoever happens to know one and has the time: a maintainer, another
user, or nobody. There is no response-time commitment. ojAlgo is MIT licensed, and the
work behind it is volunteered.

## Commercial

[Optimatika](https://www.optimatika.se/), the company behind ojAlgo, offers commercial
support — private, prioritised, and direct from the people who develop the library.

If ojAlgo is in production and the public channels are not enough,
[get in touch](https://www.optimatika.se/contact/).

## ojAlgo stays MIT

Nothing here changes the licence. ojAlgo is MIT, it has been since 2003, and it stays
that way. Commercial support buys attention, prioritisation and commitment. It does
not buy permission — you already have that.
